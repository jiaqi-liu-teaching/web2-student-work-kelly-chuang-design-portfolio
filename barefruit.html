PRODUCT CAMPAIGN: BAREFRUIT

SUMMARY

This project explores a large-scale advertising campaign for a national product, created without budget constraints to prioritize bold, impactful design.

BareFruit is all about pure, honest ingredients — no added sugar, no weird stuff, just 100% fruit. Our mission is to strip it back to what matters: fresh, vibrant flavor straight from nature. We stand for transparency, simplicity, and the joy of fruit in its truest form. BareFruit is juice, unfiltered — the fruit, the whole fruit, nothing but the fruit.
